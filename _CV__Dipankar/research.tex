\makerubrichead{Summary Statement}
Robotics researcher working at the intersection of soft robotics, cable-driven systems, deformable-object manipulation, control, and machine learning. My research translates modelling, perception, and control methods into robotic technologies for healthcare, rehabilitation, garment manufacturing, and inspection. My doctoral work produced RoSE, an early soft-robotic esophagus for controlled endoprosthetic stent testing (\textit{Soft Robotics}, 2021), validated through industry-funded work (BvW Holding, NZD 39,000, 2018); RoSE is cited in subsequent biomimetic esophageal motility research (\textit{Nature Communications}, 2026). As a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellow at Imperial College London, I am developing cable-driven soft reconfigurable exoskeletons for muscle-impairment rehabilitation, building on this soft-robotics and control foundation, cable-driven parallel robots, fabric manipulation, and learning-based control.
