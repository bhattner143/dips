March 12th, 2026 | Research Milestone — RoSE Foundation Cited in Nature Communications 🌟

A milestone for my doctoral research direction: Kilroy et al. published “A Soft-Robotic Biomimetic Benchtop Model for Esophageal Motility Simulation” in Nature Communications (12 March 2026), presenting the RoboGullet+ platform for esophageal motility, stent migration, achalasia simulation, and bolus-transit studies. [Nature paper]

RoSE (my work, 2021): An early pneumatic soft-robotic esophagus for controlled in vitro endoprosthetic stent testing—programmable peristaltic waves, stent radial-force and migration measurement, and manometry-based swallow signatures. [RoSE DOI] [arXiv] [Project Website]

RoboGullet+ (2026): Builds on this foundation by citing RoSE and adopting its circumferential pneumatic design, while advancing the field with independent longitudinal–circumferential muscle actuation, biohybrid porcine tissue lining, and simulation of normal and diseased motility (including achalasia types I–III). The 2026 study reports, for example, that stent migration can increase more than fivefold when longitudinal muscle movement is included versus circumferential actuation alone—highlighting why the earlier RoSE focus on physiologically structured benchtop stent evaluation remains scientifically relevant.
